Health

Mountain Pose: Stand tall with feet hip-width apart and palms facing forward (“I stand firm like a mountain”).

Downward-Facing Dog: Start on hands and knees, then press your hips up toward the ceiling to make an upside-down "V" shape. You can wag your tail like a friendly dog

Cat-Cow Pose: Alternate between arching your back up toward the sky (Cat) and letting your belly drop down gently while looking up (Cow).

Pose: Stand on one foot, place the sole of your other foot on your ankle or calf, and reach your branches (arms) up high.

Health tips
